# Budgeting and savings

## Budgets
Set a monthly budget per category (for example Groceries or Eating out). You receive a notification when you reach 80% and 100% of the budget. Budgets reset on the first day of every month.

## Savings goals
Create a savings goal with a target amount and date. Auto-save can round up every card payment to the nearest euro and move the difference to your goal.

## Interest
Savings accounts earn interest that is calculated daily and paid out monthly. The current rate is shown in the app under Savings > Interest.
