# Cards

## Freezing a card
You can freeze a card instantly from the app: open Cards, tap the card and choose "Freeze". A frozen card declines all new payments, but subscriptions already authorised may still be retried later. Unfreeze it at any time from the same screen.

## Lost or stolen card
If your card is lost or stolen, freeze it first, then choose "Replace card". A new card with a new number is shipped within 3-5 business days. Your virtual card is available immediately so you can keep paying online.

## Card limits
The default daily ATM withdrawal limit is EUR 500 and the default daily card payment limit is EUR 2,500. You can change both limits under Card settings > Limits. Increases above the maximum require identity re-verification.
