# Payments

## SEPA transfers
SEPA transfers within the eurozone are free. Standard SEPA transfers arrive within one business day; SEPA Instant transfers arrive within 10 seconds, 24/7, when the receiving bank supports them.

## Disputing a card payment
If you don't recognise a card payment, first check whether the merchant name differs from the shop name. To dispute it, open the payment and tap "Report a problem". Disputes must be filed within 120 days of the transaction date. Resolution usually takes up to 45 days.

## Scheduled payments
You can schedule one-off or recurring payments. If your balance is too low on the execution date, the payment is retried once the next day and then cancelled.
