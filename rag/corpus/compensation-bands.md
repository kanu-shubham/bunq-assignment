# Compensation Bands 2026

## How bands work

Each level has a band with a minimum, midpoint, and maximum. Offers are made
between the minimum and the midpoint; above the midpoint requires People Ops
approval and a written justification.

## Engineering bands (Amsterdam, EUR, annual base)

| Level | Minimum | Midpoint | Maximum |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Engineer I | 52,000 | 62,000 | 72,000 |
| Engineer II | 68,000 | 80,000 | 92,000 |
| Senior Engineer | 88,000 | 104,000 | 120,000 |
| Staff Engineer | 115,000 | 134,000 | 153,000 |
| Principal Engineer | 145,000 | 168,000 | 191,000 |

## Standby rate

On-call standby is paid at EUR 250 per week for a primary rotation and EUR 100
per week for secondary, independent of level.

## Review cycle

Bands are reviewed annually in Q1 against market data. Individual position
within a band is reviewed at the same time; a band change does not automatically
change anyone's pay.
