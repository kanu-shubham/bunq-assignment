# Engineering Onboarding

## Day one

Your laptop is waiting at the front desk. Sign in with the temporary credentials
in your welcome email, then enrol a hardware security key before anything else —
you cannot access production systems without one.

## Repository access

Access is granted per team, not per person. Ask your onboarding buddy to add you
to the team's group in the access portal; the group grants the repositories, the
CI project, and the staging environment together. Individual repository grants
are not issued and requests for them are declined.

Your first pull request should be a documentation fix. It exercises the whole
path — branch, CI, review, merge — without risking anything.

## Working hours and office

We expect everyone in the Amsterdam office **four days a week**, with Friday as
the standard remote day. Talk to your manager if your situation needs something
different.

## Travel and conferences

New engineers are budgeted for one conference in their first year. Booking goes
through TravelDesk and the expense rules in the Expense and Travel Policy apply
in full, including the requirement to get manager approval before booking.

## Getting help

`#eng-help` is staffed during business hours. For anything that looks like a
security problem, do not debug it in `#eng-help` — declare an incident instead.
