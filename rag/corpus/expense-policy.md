# Expense and Travel Policy

## Scope

This policy covers all employees and contractors of Acme N.V. It does not cover
capital equipment purchases, which follow the procurement process.

## Submitting a claim

Expense claims must be submitted within **30 days** of the date the expense was
incurred. Claims submitted after 30 days require written approval from your
department head, and claims older than 90 days will not be reimbursed under any
circumstances.

Every claim needs an itemised receipt. A card statement line is not a receipt.
For expenses under EUR 25 a photo of the receipt is sufficient; above EUR 25 the
original must also be filed in Workday.

## Approval thresholds

| Amount (EUR) | Approver | Turnaround |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 - 250 | Line manager | 2 business days |
| 251 - 2,000 | Department head | 5 business days |
| 2,001 - 10,000 | Finance business partner | 10 business days |
| Above 10,000 | CFO | Case by case |

## Per diem rates

Per diem covers meals and incidentals. It is paid for each full day away from
your home city, and at 50% for departure and return days.

| City | Per diem (EUR) | Hotel cap (EUR/night) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Amsterdam | 65 | 210 |
| Berlin | 60 | 190 |
| London | 80 | 260 |
| New York | 95 | 320 |
| Remote / other | 55 | 170 |

## Booking travel

All flights and hotels must be booked through the TravelDesk portal. Bookings
made outside TravelDesk are reimbursed only up to the price TravelDesk quoted
for the equivalent itinerary on the day of booking.

Economy class is the default for flights under six hours. Flights over six hours
may be booked in premium economy with department head approval.

## Conference attendance

Conference tickets are an expense, not a training budget item. Register your
attendance in the Learning portal before booking, and attach the confirmation to
the claim. Managers approve conference attendance before any booking is made.
