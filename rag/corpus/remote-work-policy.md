# Remote Work Policy

## Office attendance

From 1 July 2026, the company standard is **three days per week in the office**
for all roles based within commuting distance of an Acme office. Teams choose
which three days, and publish them so that meetings can be planned around them.

This supersedes any earlier team-level or handbook guidance about four days in
the office.

## Fully remote roles

A small number of roles are designated fully remote at hire. That designation is
recorded in Workday and does not change when a team changes its office days.

## Working from abroad

You may work from another country for up to 20 working days per calendar year
without a formal assignment. Beyond 20 days there are tax and social-security
consequences and People Ops must approve in advance. Countries on the restricted
list are excluded regardless of duration.

## Equipment

The home-office allowance is EUR 750 per employee, refreshed every three years.
It is claimed through the normal expense process and is subject to the same
30-day submission deadline as any other claim.
