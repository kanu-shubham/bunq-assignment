# Expenses and travel

## Principle

Spend it as if it were your own money and you were slightly embarrassed about
it. That principle resolves most questions this page does not.

## Reimbursement

Submit in the expense tool within 30 days, with a receipt for anything above
25 EUR. Reimbursement lands with the next payroll run if approved before the
cut-off, which is the 20th of the month.

## Travel

Book through the travel tool. Economy for flights under six hours, premium
economy above. Rail is preferred over air where the journey is under five hours,
including for cost comparisons that come out marginally against it.

Hotels up to 180 EUR per night in most cities, 250 EUR in Amsterdam, London,
Zurich, and New York. Above that, get it approved before booking, not after.

## Meals

Meals during travel are reimbursed at actuals up to 60 EUR per day. Alcohol is
not reimbursed except at an approved team event.

## Home office

A one-off 500 EUR budget for desk, chair, and peripherals in your first month,
and 250 EUR every two years after. Laptops, monitors, and phones are company
equipment issued by IT, not an expense you claim.
