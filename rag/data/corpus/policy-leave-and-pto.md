# Leave, holiday and time off

## Annual leave

Full-time employees receive 27 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, plus
public holidays in your country of employment. Leave accrues monthly and is
requested in the people portal; your manager approves. Up to five unused days
carry into the next year and expire on 31 March.

We expect people to take their leave. A manager whose team consistently ends the
year with large unused balances is not running a busy team, they are running an
unsustainable one.

## Sick leave

Report sickness to your manager as early as you can on the day. There is no
certificate requirement for the first seven consecutive days. Sick days do not
come out of your annual leave and are not counted against you.

## Parental leave

Birthing parents receive 16 weeks fully paid; the non-birthing parent or partner
receives 10 weeks fully paid, which may be taken flexibly within the first year.
Adoption and surrogacy are treated identically. Statutory entitlements in your
country apply on top where they are more generous, never instead.

## Unpaid and study leave

Unpaid leave and study leave are available by agreement with your manager and
people team. Study leave for a qualification relevant to your role is normally
approved and comes with a budget contribution.
